Why we built this
Why we built this

Every minute spent searching for a bed is a minute not spent on care

MediBed Tracker exists to close the gap between a medical emergency and the nearest hospital that can actually help — with a real bed ready, right now.

The problem

During a medical emergency, families lose precious time calling or visiting hospital after hospital just to ask one question: do you have a bed? Without a shared source of truth for General, ICU, Oxygen, NICU, and Emergency beds, that search causes delayed treatment, wasted travel, added stress, and poor coordination between hospitals, patients, and ambulances.

What we're working toward

Real-time visibility into hospital bed availability
Help patients and families find the nearest hospital with an open bed
Give ambulance crews what they need to choose the right hospital, fast
Cut the delay between emergency and treatment
Improve communication between hospitals and the public
Be a reliable, easy-to-use system people can trust under pressure

How it's meant to work

1
Hospitals register
Hospitals sign up and are verified by an administrator before going live on the platform.
2
Beds are kept current
Each hospital updates its own bed availability through a secure dashboard, so the data comes from the source.
3
Search by need, not guesswork
Patients, families, or ambulance crews search by location or the specific bed type they need.
4
Get there directly
The system shows nearby hospitals with beds available, their contact details, and turn-by-turn directions via Google Maps.

What this unlocks

  • Saves valuable time during emergencies
  • Cuts unnecessary travel between hospitals
  • Better coordination between hospitals, patients, and ambulances
  • One reliable, up-to-date source for healthcare availability
  • Faster, better-informed emergency response

What's next

Real-time bed availability Live ambulance tracking Online appointment booking

Where the data comes from

Hospital details are grounded in trusted references — the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission, the National Health Portal, the Open Government Data Platform India, the Maharashtra Public Health Department, and hospitals' own official listings. Live bed counts aren't published anywhere publicly today, so the long-term plan is for each registered hospital to keep its own status current through a secure login, which is what the Hospital Login dashboard on this site previews.
